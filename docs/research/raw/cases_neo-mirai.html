Designing with ImpeccableCase study

Neo Mirai: from mockup to working site.

Neo Mirai began with two generated images: a page mockup and a brand toolkit for a fictional conference. Impeccable turned those references into a responsive website, then refined it through browser checks and adjustments.

Explore the siteTry this workflow

The page mockup and brand toolkit were generated with OpenAI GPT Image 2.

What changed

The design became a page people can use.

Keep the composition

The site carries over the mockup’s large opening artwork, dark agenda section, orange manifesto band, installation grid, and ticket section.

Prepare the images

The city scene, speaker portraits, pine overlay, and supporting illustrations were regenerated or separated into assets the page could use.

Refine it in the browser

Browser checks after each pass caught overlaps, awkward image crops, navigation states, and speaker carousel issues. Further adjustments made the layout work on small and large screens.
